Turn the agreed design from this conversation into atomic development tickets. Write them to `TICKETS.md` in the repo root (append if it exists, continuing the numbering).

Each ticket:
- `## T-<n>: <imperative title>`
- **Goal:** one sentence
- **Acceptance criteria:** checklist
- **Depends on:** ticket IDs or "none"

Keep each ticket small enough to finish and test in one sitting. Order by dependency.
