# Copy rules

## 1. Banned words
Never use: revolutionize, seamless, empower, elevate, unleash, robust, supercharge, next-gen, game-changer, "unlock your potential". Also avoid their variants (seamlessly, empowering, elevated…).

## 2. No throat-clearing
- Never open with scene-setting ("In today's fast-paced digital world…").
- Never announce the answer ("Here is your updated code…"). Start with the point.

## 3. Concrete over vague
- Lead with the mechanism and a metric: "Deploy in 30 seconds", not "Blazing fast deployments".
- If you have no real number, describe the mechanism plainly. Never invent metrics.
- Write like a direct technical editor: short sentences, active voice.

## 4. Fit the space
- Buttons: 1–3 words, verb first ("Save draft", "Export CSV").
- Labels: noun phrases, no trailing colon needed in modern forms.
- Errors: say what happened and how to fix it, in one line where possible ("Card expired. Use another card.").
- Check that text won't wrap awkwardly in its container at mobile width.

## Self-check before finishing
- [ ] No banned words
- [ ] First sentence carries information
- [ ] Every claim is a mechanism or a real number
- [ ] Short strings fit their containers
