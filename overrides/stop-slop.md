## Global rules override (claude-toolkit)

In addition to the patterns above, always remove these banned words and their variants: revolutionize, seamless, empower, elevate, unleash, robust, supercharge, next-gen, game-changer, "unlock your potential". Rewrite the sentence around a concrete mechanism or a real number instead of swapping in a synonym. The full copy rules are in `~/.claude/claude-toolkit-rules.md`.
