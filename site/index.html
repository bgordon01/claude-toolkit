With Frontier

YouBuild a ticketing system so my team can track work.

ClaudeBefore I build: can two people own a ticket? Who can close it? Where do tickets go when someone leaves?

YouOne owner. Only the owner closes. Reassign them to me.

ClaudePlan written and split into 4 pieces. The board is built in a separate copy, with checks that pass. Keep it?

Every decision made before a line of code.

Nothing changes without your confirmation. Each piece is built in a separate copy until you keep it.

Six steps, handed to you one at a time.

You don't memorise these. Say start here and Frontier hands over each one. Tap a command to copy it.

  1. 01

    Question

    Hard questions until every decision about your idea is made. Nothing is written yet.

  2. 02

    Plan

    Your answers, written up as a one-page plan (a spec) where you already keep work: GitHub, GitLab, Jira, Linear or plain files.

  3. 03

    Split

    The plan cut into small chunks of work (tickets) you can build and check one at a time.

  4. 04

    Build

    One chunk at a time. Claude writes a check (a test) first, then the code, and re-runs every check before the work is marked as done.

  5. 05

    Polish

    Screens checked (a critique) and fixed. Words stripped of anything that sounds machine-written.

  6. 06

    Review

    The change checked against the plan, then handed to you to keep or send back.
